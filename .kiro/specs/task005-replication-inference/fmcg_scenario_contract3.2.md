# TASK_005 Fictional FMCG Scenario Contract 3.2

**Status:** frozen before any scenario-3.2 LLM output or engineering-pilot result.

## Product and population

The focal brand is **VerdantCo Oat**, a wholly fictional oat-milk brand. The
target population is regular plant-based-milk category buyers. The model studies
short-run repeat brand choice during a 30-day reputation crisis; it does not
represent durable-good adoption, the general population or Oatly's actual
customers and market share.

The 20 cognitive agents form a balanced engineering panel for mechanism coverage,
not a representative consumer sample. Each persona must state:

- green-orientation segment;
- category-purchase frequency stratum;
- prior focal-brand relationship (loyal, repertoire buyer or non-user);
- price sensitivity;
- retail-availability friction;
- social posting role.

Population proportions and numeric coefficients are not estimable from this
balanced panel. External data or prospectively frozen sensitivity analysis is
required for population claims.

## Event and interventions

- Tick 5: a fictional minority-investment controversy challenges the consistency
  of VerdantCo Oat's sustainability identity.
- Communication content: rational evidence versus emotional empathy. Both
  versions must contain the same seven stipulated facts; only framing and
  emphasis may differ.
- Communication timing: Tick 6 versus Tick 10.
- Communication channel: equal-budget Hub versus Random network seeding, with
  actual reach audited separately.
- Conversion support: absent versus a Tick 6--19 price-protection voucher plus
  verified store-availability service.

Conversion support is crossed offline with each of the nine LLM communication
conditions. It does not duplicate LLM calls. Its exclusion restriction is
explicit: it changes PBC only and cannot directly change trust, Att or SN. This
restriction is a model assumption that must be sensitivity-tested.

## Cognitive correction

Subjective norm is perceived social approval, not the valence of any message.
The semantic schema must add `perceived_peer_approval` in [0, 1], defined only
from actual social-feed observations. When no social message is observed, SN is
unchanged. Global news and enterprise statements cannot update SN directly.

The LLM continues to appraise semantic valence, arousal, credibility, evidence,
topic relevance, empathy and hypocrisy. It does not directly decide purchase.

## Purchase-demand layer

Scenario 3.2 adopts the audited v3.1 separation of treatment-invariant category
opportunity, extended-TPB intention, conditional brand choice, loyalty state and
facilitation-through-PBC. The cumulative-ever-purchased endpoint remains retired.

Primary managerial outcomes are expected and realised focal-brand choice share,
incremental choices per 1,000 category occasions, recovery time, and the
communication-by-facilitation interaction. All are model outputs, not observed
sales.
