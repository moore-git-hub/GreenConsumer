# TASK_005 Purchase-Mechanism Redesign Contract 3.0

**Status:** frozen before producing any v3 replay output.

## Why the v2 purchase endpoint is retired

The v2 endpoint `PURCHASE_RATE_T30` is the cumulative proportion of agents that
ever produced a buy event during Ticks 1--30. It is not a defensible causal
purchase endpoint for the crisis-response experiment because:

1. buy events occur before the scandal at Tick 5 and before either clarification;
2. every not-yet-buying agent receives a new buy trial on every Tick;
3. the first success is absorbing (`ever_purchased=True`), although the coded
   Oatly scenario describes an existing-customer, repeat-purchase category;
4. the endpoint mixes pre-treatment and post-treatment behavior and develops a
   high control-group ceiling;
5. the code multiplies purchase intention by a daily opportunity hazard and then
   treats the result as a purchase probability, conflating category opportunity
   with conditional focal-brand choice.

The completed real-LLM v2 pilot remains historical diagnostic evidence. It is
not a calibration sample for choosing v3 coefficients and is not reusable in a
v3 formal analysis.

## Scientific objective

Separate three constructs that were conflated in v2:

1. an exogenous, condition-invariant category-purchase opportunity;
2. the probability of choosing the focal brand conditional on that opportunity;
3. the realized focal-brand choice generated by frozen common random numbers.

The redesign seeks construct validity and an auditable management interpretation.
It does not guarantee statistical significance or a five-percentage-point effect.

## Frozen v3 core

- Current implementation context: an FMCG repeat-purchase scenario (Oatly), with
  one simulation Tick interpreted as one day.
- Opportunity process: each agent has a deterministic weekly phase derived only
  from the block simulation seed and agent ID; post-crisis opportunities recur
  every 7 Ticks.
- Treatment invariance: opportunity timing cannot depend on content, channel,
  timing condition, semantic output, trust, intention, or prior focal-brand choice.
- Conditional choice probability: equal to the explicit TPB purchase intention
  at the opportunity Tick; it is not multiplied by a per-Tick opportunity rate.
- Repeat behavior: focal-brand choice is not absorbing. An agent may make another
  category choice at a later opportunity.
- Common random numbers: the brand-choice draw depends only on block seed, agent
  ID, Tick and the fixed action label `brand_choice_v3`.
- Primary purchase estimand candidate: condition mean of expected focal-brand
  choice probability across post-crisis purchase opportunities.
- Secondary behavioral estimand candidate: realized focal-brand choice share
  across the same opportunities.
- All pre-crisis choices are excluded from the post-crisis estimands.

The expected-probability endpoint is a model-implied expected conversion measure,
not observed sales data. The realized-choice endpoint remains necessary as a
behavioral robustness outcome.

## Communication versus conversion support

Content, channel and response timing are reputation-repair interventions. They
may affect trust, Att and SN, but they must not be made to change PBC merely to
manufacture purchases.

A later, separately identified enterprise action may target purchase friction
(for example, price support, trial access or availability) through PBC. Any such
factor requires:

- a frozen stimulus and exposure rule;
- an externally justified or empirically calibrated PBC update;
- communication-only, facilitation-only, combined and no-action comparisons;
- a pre-specified interaction estimand;
- no coefficient selection using p-values or desired effect size.

No default PBC lift is authorized by this contract.

## Scenario boundary

This weekly repeat-purchase process is not transferable to a new-energy-vehicle
adoption model. If the thesis's final empirical scenario is an NEV purchase, the
opportunity process, ownership state, replacement cycle and adoption endpoint
must be redesigned before any formal run. Oatly/FMCG output cannot be described
as NEV purchase evidence.

## Integrity firewall

- no winner is hard-coded;
- no coefficient may be changed until `p < .05`;
- no v2 real-LLM row may enter v3 formal inference;
- the first v3 replay is descriptive and computes no p-values;
- a new independent engineering pilot, sensitivity plan, MDE, power analysis,
  seed ledger and execution authorization are required before v3 formal inference.

## Required sequence

Contract freeze -> parallel implementation -> offline mechanism tests -> frozen
v2-trajectory replay for construct diagnosis only -> scenario decision -> empirical
calibration or prospectively frozen sensitivity ranges -> new engineering pilot ->
new variance/OC/power -> new formal authorization -> independent formal run.
