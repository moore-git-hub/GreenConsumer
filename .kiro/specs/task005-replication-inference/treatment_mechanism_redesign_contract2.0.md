# TASK_005 Treatment-Mechanism Redesign Contract 2.0

**Status:** frozen before inspecting any post-amendment outcome.

## Scientific objective

The redesign is intended to make enterprise interventions causally identifiable,
persistent enough to be managerially meaningful, and prospectively well powered.
It is **not** permitted to tune the model repeatedly until a desired p-value appears.

## Core diagnosis

The current implementation structurally compresses treatment-control differences through
mechanical return-to-baseline behavior, cluster-fixed clarification anchor lifting, limited
direct enterprise reach, and direct LLM binary behavior generation. It also does not yet
fully operationalize the thesis chain of semantic interpretation -> TPB psychological state
(Att/SN/PBC) + emotion/trust -> intention -> behavior -> network diffusion.

## Frozen redesign requirements

- Semantic parsing: valence, arousal, credibility, evidence strength, topic relevance/consistency,
  hypocrisy perception.
- Psychological state: trust, Att, SN, PBC, emotion, crisis memory, repair memory.
- Decision: explicit purchase/posting intention; final behavior uses frozen simulation RNG.
- Content: Rational and Empathy enter different psychological channels; no winner is hard-coded.
- Channel: Hub/Random differ through real topology/reach under the same budget.
- Timing: Immediate/Delayed differ through the pre-intervention state of crisis/social memory.
- Control: no clarification, and time passing alone must not equal successful trust repair.

## Literature integrity

Only source-supported claims may be attributed to literature. Numeric coefficients that are not
empirically identified must be labeled model assumptions and sensitivity-tested. The prior
Forrester report supports consumer heterogeneity, not arbitrary numeric coefficients or copied
population shares. The previously discussed 米加宁 et al. source and Bail (2024) PNAS source
must not be quoted or given page-specific claims until their exact records/full text are
re-identified.
